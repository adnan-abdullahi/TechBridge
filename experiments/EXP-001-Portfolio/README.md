# EXP-001 — Portfolio Experiment

**Status:** Active  
**Type:** Real-world learning and problem-solving experiment  
**Role:** Evidence-generating laboratory

## Purpose

This experiment uses the development of my professional portfolio as a real-world problem-solving and learning environment for discovering and validating processes that TechBridge may eventually support.

The portfolio is not being treated merely as a website to build. It is being used to investigate uncertainty, capability, decisions, evaluation, gaps, and sufficient clarity while generating evidence for the future TechBridge platform.

## Initial Problem

> “I genuinely lack a clear way to present myself professionally to a stranger asking who I am in relation to my work.”

The deeper problem concerns the absence of a clear communication system through which another person can understand my identity, work, professional background, engineering approach, and supporting evidence.

## Investigation

The attempt to visually represent myself exposed a deeper issue: I had not yet clearly defined what a stranger needs to understand about me or the journey they may take when encountering my portfolio.

A visitor's intent cannot be assumed. Different visitors may arrive with different intentions, including curiosity, evaluation, collaboration, learning, or seeking opportunities.

The current working direction is therefore to provide relevant context and evidence rather than attempting to prescribe a predetermined conclusion.

## Emerging Evidence Structure

The current provisional evidence structure consists of:

1. **Identity**
2. **Professional Background**
3. **Engineering Approach**
4. **Project Evidence**

The portfolio itself is a project and can serve as primary evidence. Other projects may be used where additional evidence is needed to support a particular understanding.

This structure remains provisional and must be tested against evidence.

## Evidence Principle

The portfolio should not depend primarily on claims that the visitor is expected to accept.

Instead, the aim is to put relevant evidence and context in front of the visitor and allow them to connect the evidence and form their own judgment.

Different visitors may reach different conclusions or outcomes because they may have different purposes for visiting. The portfolio therefore needs to provide a truthful and useful foundation for independent interpretation.

## Current Working Cycle

**Understand → Push Forward Under Uncertainty → Evaluate → Identify Gaps → Iterate → Establish Sufficient Clarity**

“Sufficient clarity” does not mean complete certainty. It means having enough understanding to make a responsible next decision while acknowledging what remains uncertain.

This is currently a working hypothesis and has not yet been established as a final TechBridge doctrine.

## Current Uncertainties

- What amount and type of evidence is sufficient?
- What evidence is relevant across different visitor types?
- How much context is necessary for independent understanding?
- Can different visitors independently discover what is relevant to them?
- How can the portfolio support different visitor intentions without prescribing a conclusion?
- Are the four emerging evidence dimensions sufficient?
- What additional evidence may be required?
- What should intentionally remain uncertain?

## Current Decisions

- Do not implement before understanding the problem.
- Do not assume visitor intent.
- Do not prescribe the conclusion a visitor should reach.
- Put relevant evidence and context in front of the visitor.
- Allow visitors to form their own judgments from the evidence.
- Treat the portfolio itself as a project and a source of evidence.
- Use other projects as supporting evidence where necessary.
- Keep decisions evidence-driven.
- Preserve the reasoning and history of the experiment in the repository.
- Do not design the TechBridge platform from assumptions generated by this experiment alone.

## What Remains Unsolved

The central unresolved question is:

> What body and structure of evidence allows different people, with different intentions, to independently understand who I am professionally and form an informed judgment?

A second unresolved question is how to organize that evidence without prescribing the conclusion the visitor should reach.

## Relationship to TechBridge

This experiment is not the TechBridge platform.

It is a laboratory for discovering and validating parts of the human problem-solving and learning process that TechBridge may eventually support.

The broader working relationship is:

**Real problem → Investigation → Evidence → Learning → Changed understanding → Action → Evaluation → Improved capability**

No platform architecture, agentic behavior, technology stack, or implementation decisions are being established from this experiment at this stage.
